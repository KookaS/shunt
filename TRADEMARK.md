# Trademark Policy

Shunt is **Apache-2.0** — the router core is free for everyone, forever, and the
license never restricts what you can do with the code. That permissiveness is what
makes adoption possible. It also means the **code license alone cannot defend the
Shunt name**: anyone can legally build on or host Apache-2.0 code, and trademark is
the legal tool that protects identity claims, not code use. The trademark — **not
the license** — is the moat.

## What we claim

"Shunt" and "shunt-router" identify this project's products and services. We own the
name across the channels we publish under (GitHub organization, package registries,
domain). Using the name to identify **this** project or to point at it is welcome;
using it to claim a relationship that does not exist is not.

## No CLA on the core

Contributions to the core are made under the **DCO** (Developer Certificate of
Origin), never a contributor license agreement. You keep the copyright to what you
contribute and certify you have the right to submit it under the project's Apache-2.0
license. There is no CLA because there is nothing a CLA would protect against: the
core is permissively licensed and never retro-restricted.

## Acceptable use

You may freely use the Shunt name and the project's logos to:

- **Run, deploy, and modify** Shunt for any purpose — this is what the license grants.
- **Host or redistribute** Shunt under the Apache-2.0 license, provided you preserve
  the license and notices, and do not misrepresent your offering.
- **Refer to Shunt factually** in your own product's documentation (e.g. "integrates
  with Shunt" or "built on Shunt") where that reference is accurate.

## Unacceptable use

You may not:

- **Claim or imply official status** — e.g. calling your fork or service "the official
  Shunt", "Shunt Cloud", "endorsed by Shunt", or "the Shunt team" when it is not.
- **Use the Shunt name or logos in a way that suggests** the project authorizes,
  sponsors, or is otherwise affiliated with you, without written permission.
- **Register or squat** the Shunt name or confusingly similar marks (domains, package
  names, org names, social handles) to impersonate the project or capture its traffic.

These restrictions apply to **identity claims**, not to code use. Forking, modifying,
redistributing, and self-hosting the code remain fully permitted by the Apache-2.0
license regardless of this policy.

## Forks, derivatives, and services

If you fork Shunt or run a service on top of it, you may describe what you built
accurately, but pick a name that does not claim to be Shunt. "A fork of Shunt" and
"powered by Shunt" describe a relationship; "Shunt" alone as your product's name
describes identity — and identity is what the mark protects.

## Questions

If you want to use the Shunt name or marks in a way not covered here — a partnership,
a service, or a name that sits close to the mark — open an issue or discussion in the
repository and ask. Permission for a specific use is case by case; this project is a
solo-maintained open-source project, and the default answer is to say yes whenever
the use is honest.

## Reporting

If you see the Shunt name used in a way that misleads people into thinking a service
or fork is official Shunt, report it via the repository's security channel or open a
GitHub issue.
